Potential plays

Recommendations for Week 10 based on the original model. Schedule refresh updates matchups and final scores, but does not update team records, player stats, or model probabilities. These are not sportsbook odds.

Team TD, passing and rushing picks are estimates against standard reference lines. Player picks use season totals from StatsCrew league leaders (Pro-Football-Reference blocks automated access), so only leading QBs, RBs and TD scorers appear, lines are each player’s season average, and TD counts may miss receiving scores for backs. Odds shown are model-estimated (about 4.5% margin built in), and spread/total lines are model reference lines, not sportsbook prices. Check your book for real lines before betting. These are estimates, not guarantees; same-game legs are correlated, so books price them differently. Please bet responsibly.
